# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in this project respectful, inclusive and professional for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience level or role.

## Expected behaviour

- Be constructive, precise and respectful.
- Focus criticism on ideas, code, evidence and assumptions.
- Welcome questions from newcomers.
- Acknowledge uncertainty and correct mistakes transparently.
- Respect confidentiality, safety constraints and intellectual property.
- Avoid publishing sensitive industrial or personal information.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, unsafe technical advice, disclosure of private information and repeated bad-faith conduct are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate this code, restrict participation or report serious conduct through the appropriate platform channels.

## Scope

This code applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, community spaces and project-related communication.
